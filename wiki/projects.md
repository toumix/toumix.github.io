# Projects

## DisCoPy

- **What**: Python toolkit for computing with string diagrams and monoidal categories.
- **URL**: https://discopy.org  |  https://github.com/discopy/discopy
- **Docs**: https://docs.discopy.org
- **Role**: Main developer (since ~2019).
- **v1.0 released**: December 2022.
- **Key capabilities**: free monoidal categories, functors, tensor networks, quantum circuits, bicategories, Markov categories, hypergraphs.
- **Used for**: QNLP pipelines, functorial learning, diagrammatic differentiation, probabilistic dataflow, active inference demos.

## lambeq

- **What**: High-level Python library for Quantum NLP (built on top of DisCoPy).
- **URL**: https://github.com/CQCL/lambeq
- **Role**: Contributor and co-designer (Quantinuum / Cambridge Quantum).
- **Reference**: [arXiv:2110.04236](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.04236)

## Quantum NLP on photonic hardware

- **What**: Experimental quantum-enhanced kernel-based machine learning on a photonic processor.
- **Published**: Nature Photonics (2025), pages 1-8.
- **Collaborators**: Z. Yin, I. Agresti, G. de Felice, D. Brown, C. Pentangelo, S. Piacentini, A. Crespi, F. Ceccarelli, R. Osellame, B. Coecke, P. Walther.
- **DOI**: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41566-025-01682-5
